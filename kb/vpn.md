Symptoms: VPN client shows "Connection failed", "Timed out", or connects but internal sites don't load.

Make sure regular internet works first — open any public website.
Disconnect and reconnect the VPN client.
Restart the VPN client app completely (right-click tray icon > Exit, then reopen).
Check that your date and time are correct (wrong time breaks VPN certificates).
Restart the computer.
If you use a hotel or public Wi-Fi, accept the Wi-Fi login page first, then connect VPN.
If it still fails, escalate with a ticket and include the exact error message.
